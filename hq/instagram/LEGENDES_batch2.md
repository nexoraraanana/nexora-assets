Night vision: black & white or colour? 🌙

Most security cameras switch to infrared at night: they see in total darkness, but in black & white. Colour night vision models keep the colours of clothes and cars, but they need a little light, or switch on a soft white light when it gets too dark.

The right choice depends on the spot: dark garden, lit street, parking…

## c05 — Carrousel · How many days does your recorder keep?

How many days does your recorder keep? 📼

It depends on 3 things:
1️⃣ The number of cameras
2️⃣ Resolution & compression (H.265 needs less space than H.264)
3️⃣ The hard drive size

Example: 4 cameras · 4 MP · H.265 · 2 TB ≈ 1 to 3 weeks of continuous recording. That's an estimate: it depends on the bitrate and on how busy the scene is. Motion-only recording lasts longer.

We size the storage for how long you need to keep your footage.

## c06 — Carrousel · Cat5e, Cat6 or Cat6A?

Cat5e, Cat6 or Cat6A? 🔌

• Cat5e: 1 Gbps up to 100 m
• Cat6: 1 Gbps up to 100 m, and 10 Gbps on shorter runs (up to ~55 m)
• Cat6A: 10 Gbps up to 100 m

All three can carry PoE for cameras and access points.

## c07 — Carrousel · What is PoE?

What is PoE? ⚡

Power over Ethernet: one network cable brings both power and data to a camera, an access point or many intercoms.

• 802.3af (PoE): up to 15.4 W per port
• 802.3at (PoE+): up to 30 W
• 802.3bt (PoE++): up to 60–90 W
(Power at the switch port: the device receives a little less.)

Why we use it: it can't be jammed like Wi-Fi, it runs up to 100 m per cable, and with a UPS your cameras stay on during a power cut.
